How to book

Booking your room

Booking is by email. Send a short note to Värdshuset Mariefred and quote our booking code so they know you are with the celebration and place you in the family's block of rooms.

Booking code
gripsnäs
  1. 1Email Värdshuset Mariefred quoting the code gripsnäs.
  2. 2Say which you'd like, B&B, double or single, and your arrival and departure dates.
  3. 3They'll confirm your room and the price by reply.
Email to book with code "gripsnäs"

This opens your email app with everything filled in, just add your dates and send. Prefer to call? The phone number is on the hotel's website.
