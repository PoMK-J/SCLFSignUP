When you arrive

Parking

Once you reach Gripsnäs, here's where to leave the car and how the arrival works, so the only thing on your mind is the celebration.

Free on-site parking

There is a dedicated car park at the venue with plenty of space for everyone, no charge, no booking needed. Follow the signs as you arrive and a marshal will point you to a spot.

Drop-off

Being driven? There's a turning circle right by the entrance, perfect for dropping off anyone who'd rather not walk far, before the driver parks.

Accessible spaces

A few spots are reserved right beside the entrance for anyone who needs step-free access. Just let us know when you register.

EV charging

Charging is available in Mariefred town, a few minutes away. If you'll need a charge during the evening, mention it and we'll point you to the nearest one.

Share a ride

Staying over and leaving the car? Many of the family are travelling from Stockholm, tell us and we'll happily help match lifts.
